About CampusConnect

Connecting students with campus activities

What is CampusConnect?

CampusConnect is a college event management and discovery website designed to help students discover upcoming events, workshops, competitions and activities happening on campus.

Our Purpose

The purpose of CampusConnect is to provide students with one convenient platform where they can explore events and register for activities.
